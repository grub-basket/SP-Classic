# Stashpad Classic 0.102.26 — retirement notice

Adds an in-app heads-up that **Stashpad Classic is retired** and points you to
mainline **Stashpad** (now on the Obsidian community store), so you don't have to
find out by checking the store page.

- A one-time startup notice with a **Switch to Stashpad** link. It only stops
  appearing once you actually click through or choose "Don't show again" — a reflexive
  dismissal just brings it back next launch, so the message doesn't get lost.
- A slim **Retired · Switch to Stashpad** bar at the top of the Stashpad view, always
  there while you use the plugin. Its × hides it for the session; it returns next
  launch until you've moved over.

No other changes — same frozen build. Switch here:
https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/stashpad
